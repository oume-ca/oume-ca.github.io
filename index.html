Generally Exempt from Permit (Like-for-like replacement)

Standard Switch or Receptacle Replacement

In Calgary, replacing existing light switches or outlets on a like-for-like basis generally does not require a permit, provided no new wiring is run from the panel.

Safety Note: Always de-energize the circuit at the main breaker panel and verify with a non-contact voltage tester before touching internal conductors.
Reference: City of Calgary Homeowner Electrical Guidelines & Safety Codes
