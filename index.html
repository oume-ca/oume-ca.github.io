Promoting Electrical Knowledge Across Calgary

Welcome to OUME! This platform was created out of a commitment to safety, transparency, and high electrical standards in Alberta. Having trained through the recognized Alberta electrical trade pathway, my focus is on ensuring homeowners understand how residential electrical systems operate.

Many homeowners in Calgary are unsure about what electrical tasks they can safely tackle themselves, what requires a municipal permit under City rules, or how extreme Alberta freeze cycles affect their home's wiring.

OUME is designed as a pure educational portal to demystify these rules, offer clear non-technical guides, and keep Calgary families safe from common electrical hazards.
